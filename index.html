About Me

Hi, I'm Mohammad Marufur Rahman, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at University of Texas at Dallas (UTD). I work in Human Language Technology Research Institute (HLTRI) under the supervision of Dr. Sanda M. Harabagiu. My research primarily focuses on the areas of large language models, natural language processing, and trustworthy AI. Prior joining UTD, I completed my master's degree in Computer Science at Wake Forest University, where I was advised by Dr. Grey Ballard. I worked on sensitivity and efficiency of neural computing models, specifically catastrophic forgetting and parallel algorithms for sketching large matrices.
